# Motion Working Surface

`Repo/Motion/` contains active Motion sources. Pending Motion files use `Repo/Motion/motion-{slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`. Skill identity remains lowercase `motioner`.

`motioner` owns the finite lifecycle, approval/execution boundaries, canonical landing, consumer Audit, validation, History Triage Result, and source retirement. After closeout, retain the complete Motion source under `Repo/days/<closeout-date>/Motion/`; the canonical Audit remains the current decision record.

`shaping` owns approved repo-wide path-drift and asset-placement shaping, including bounded moves under `Repo/days/YYYY-MM-DD/`. `rem-ready` owns daily `today` snapshots; `motioner` owns named Motion lifecycle and retirement. When a dated artifact's custody is unclear, route the decision to `motioner` first.

The [TeamsPage custody](../TeamsPage/README.md) is an internal review artifact surface only. Original sources, evidence, Human decisions, and writeback remain in their owning workflows.
