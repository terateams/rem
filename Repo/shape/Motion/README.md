# rem Shape Working Surface

`Repo/shape/` contains only two working directories: `Motion/` for pending Motions and `TeamsPage/` for staged non-authority Mirror Page artifacts. Skill identity remains lowercase `teamspage`.

Pending Motion files use `Motion/motion-{slug}-{date}.md`. `motioner` owns finite lifecycle, approval/execution boundaries, canonical landing, consumer Audit, validation, History Triage Result, and source retirement. After closeout, retain the complete Motion source under `Repo/days/<closeout-date>/Motion/`; the canonical Audit remains the current decision record.

`looper` owns approved path-drift and asset-placement migrations, including dated placement under `Repo/days/YYYY-MM-DD/`. `rem-ready` owns daily `today` snapshots. When a dated artifact's custody is unclear, route the decision to `motioner` first.

The [TeamsPage custody](../TeamsPage/README.md) is an internal review artifact surface only. Original sources, evidence, Human decisions, and writeback remain in their owning workflows.
